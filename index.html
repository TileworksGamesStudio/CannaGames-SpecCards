How to Play

  1. Examine the Goal: Read the prompt carefully. Slots 1 through 5 require a precise chronological, structural, or logical sequence.
  2. Select & Position: Tap an active slot, then tap any tile from the pool to place it. Tapping an already filled slot clears it and returns it to the pool.
  3. Four Attempts: Submitting verifies all 5 slots. Correct items lock permanently in place (✓). Incorrect items return to the pool.
  4. Strategic Clue: After 2 incorrect attempts, an informative hint automatically unlocks to guide your remaining tries.
